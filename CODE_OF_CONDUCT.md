# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

ThumbPin Pvt Ltd is committed to providing a respectful, harassment-free
environment for everyone who contributes to or works with this codebase,
including employees, contractors, and authorized collaborators.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully and professionally in code reviews, issues,
  commit messages, and any other project communication.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Protect the confidentiality and proprietary nature of this codebase at
  all times (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory jokes or language, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Unauthorized disclosure, copying, or distribution of this proprietary
  codebase or any confidential material related to it.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional workplace setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions related to this
repository, including code reviews, issue trackers, pull requests, and
associated internal communication channels.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to your
manager or to HR at ThumbPin Pvt Ltd. All reports will be handled
promptly and confidentially.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in corrective action up to and including removal
from the project and/or termination of engagement, at the discretion of
ThumbPin Pvt Ltd management.
