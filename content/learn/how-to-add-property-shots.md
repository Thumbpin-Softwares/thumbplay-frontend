## Overview

Thumbplay AI allows you to turn static real estate photos into high-converting video ads. By feeding clean property images into the Ad Generator, you can quickly produce dynamic promo clips, apply custom video styles, and refine the output using the Ad Editor.

Follow this guide to prepare and sequence your photos for the best generation results.

## 1. Preparing Your Property Photos

To ensure the AI generates smooth movement and crisp visuals, follow these image quality guidelines before uploading:

- **High-Resolution Quality**: Use sharp, high-resolution photos (1080p or higher) so details like flooring, countertops, and fixtures render cleanly.
- **Balanced Lighting**: Bright, evenly lit daytime photos yield the best results. Avoid dark, heavily shadowed, or backlit shots.
- **Straight Angles**: Keep camera lines straight (walls and doorframes vertical) to help the generator maintain correct spatial perspective.
- **Decluttered Spaces**: Clean, organized rooms allow the generator to highlight core property features rather than background clutter.

## 2. Choosing the Right Video Generator Styles

When uploading your images to the Ad Generator, pair them with the video template that matches your campaign goals:

- **Action Packed Property Reveal**: Ideal for high-energy property walk-throughs, highlights, and quick room transitions.
- **Model Exiting Luxury Vehicle**: Perfect for high-end luxury listings where you want to lead with an impressive, cinematic entry.
- **Frustrated Anchor**: Great for hook-based ads that address common buyer pain points before revealing the property.

## 3. Step-by-Step Workflow in Thumbplay AI

1. **Access the Ad Generator**: Go to your Dashboard and select the Ad Generator.
2. **Upload Image Batch**: Select and upload your property photos in logical walkthrough order (Exterior → Living Room → Kitchen → Bedrooms).
3. **Select Style Preset**: Choose your desired video generator concept (Action Packed Property Reveal, Model Exiting Luxury Vehicle, etc.).
4. **Edit in Ad Editor**: Once generated, open the clip in the Ad Editor to add subtitles, adjust pacing, and fine-tune your final ad before publishing.

## Conclusion

Mastering your visual pipeline is the single most effective way to elevate your real estate marketing with Thumbplay AI. By starting with crisp, well-lit photographs and structuring your upload batch strategically, you give our generator the precise data it needs to build immersive, studio-quality ads.
